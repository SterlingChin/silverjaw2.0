Same invasion. Two sides of the story. Pick your doodle.

Pencil-drawn trouble. Endless pages. One very confused cow.

Notes from mission control

Human: A / D or ← / → to run. W / ↑ / Space to jump. Aim with your mouse and hold click to fire. Without a mouse, hold J to auto-aim at the closest enemy. Collect labeled crates for a spread gun, rapid blaster, or rockets. Pick up rapid + spread in either order for a piercing laser, or rapid + rockets for rapid-fire rockets. Then laser + spread becomes a lava gun with one continuous, widening fan of lava and 1,000 fuel, and laser + rockets becomes a giant laser. Combine them before your ammo runs out. Special ammo is limited; your pencil pistol never runs out.

UFO: WASD or arrow keys to fly. Hold Space, click, or J to use the tractor beam. Hover above people and cows until they reach your ship. Collect WIDE BEAM, STRONG BEAM, SHIELD, and SPEED. Each lasts 30 seconds. Combine them for tornadoes, bubbles, vacuum dashes, and reflected bullets. Shields spend six seconds of charge to block a hit. Bubbles keep lifting after you release the beam. Tornadoes can abduct soldiers and trucks! Army fire and jets will try to stop you.

Both: Collect + pickups to heal. Finish each wave's target to advance. P / Escape pauses. Touch controls appear on phones and tablets; human FIRE automatically aims. Your best score is saved on this browser when storage is available.

Open Combo Map above for every recipe and its full pickup path.

Tip: abductions work best when you fly low and keep your target inside the beam.
